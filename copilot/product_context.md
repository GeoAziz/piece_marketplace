Kenya Land Marketplace — product context

Purpose
- Reduce information asymmetry and make land listings more transparent and harder to fraudulently portray.

Primary users
- Sellers: list land parcels and submit supporting evidence.
- Buyers: discover listings, review evidence, and contact sellers.
- Admins: review evidence, adjudicate disputes, and moderate content.

Non-goals
- The platform does NOT confirm or guarantee legal ownership, title validity, or survey accuracy.

Success metrics (initial)
- Percentage of listings with attached evidence.
- Time-to-first-admin-flag for suspicious uploads.
- Reduction in repeat suspicious actors.
