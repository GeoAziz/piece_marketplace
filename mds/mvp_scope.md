MVP scope (concise)

In scope (Stage 1)
- User registration and authentication (basic email/password, OAuth optional).
- Create and manage listings (seller flows).
- Upload evidence documents tied to listings.
- Admin evidence review workflow and badge assignment.
- Public listing pages showing metadata and badge states (no raw documents).

Out of scope (Stage 1)
- Legal/title verification with government agencies.
- Mobile native apps (deferred).
- Full-text search and heavy analytics (deferred).

Deferred
- Buyer-seller messaging and payments (Stage 2).
- Third-party verification integrations (Stage 3).
