Trust and safety summary

Principles
- Explicit: always state uncertainty and limits of evidence.
- Conservative: never use language implying legal endorsement.
- Auditable: all review actions recorded.

User-facing rules
- Badges indicate reviewed evidence only; they do not indicate legal ownership.
- When evidence is missing or insufficient, surface clear warnings and next steps for users.

Operational guardrails
- Rate limit uploads per account and per IP.
- Auto-flag suspicious patterns for admin review (e.g., many listings from same account with identical documents).
